This is a separate, one-time step for legitimate recruiters — not for workers. It uses live network verification to confirm you're really holding the phone behind the number you register, the same way a worker's check confirms signals about a number, but from your side.

Important: the next step must be completed on this phone's own mobile data connection — not WiFi, a VPN, or a hotspot. The network confirms your identity by checking which SIM your connection is actually coming from, so it can't be done from a different device or a desktop browser.
